# Demonstration script (English)

1. “We help a B2B owner finish a cash-gap decision with evidence.” Show synthetic
   120,000 CNY, payroll 80,000, rent 20,000, supplier 90,000 and receipt 100,000 later.
2. Show contractual dates, protected obligations and source hash. The calendar
   reaches -70,000; a hard 20,000 reserve makes the net need 90,000.
3. Compare no debt and financing. The late cheap product cannot solve the early gap.
   The DEMO bridge is 91,000 gross, 90,000 net, 92,000 repayment. Explain finite-grid
   optimality, independent validation and the adverse customer-delay stress.
4. Advance the synthetic clock. Review the exact recipient/amount/net/terms and
   approve one action. SUBMITTED does not change confirmed cash to “success”.
5. Observe disbursement settlement, then approve payroll. Inject a lost response.
   Show UNKNOWN and the 80,000 reserve. Recover by reading the same stable operation;
   demonstrate one settlement, without a new payment key.
6. Pay rent and supplier, observe customer receipt, then repay the debt. Show all
   five settled actions, 28,000 CNY final cash, zero open payables/debt and 0 discrepancy.
7. Close with boundaries: synthetic sandbox, mock agent, no real bank, real-model
   quality NOT_RUN, incomplete production isolation. Show the actual test report.
